<p>Google continues to accelerate its release cadence with Gemini 4 Neon, delivering a small Intelligence gain over Gemini 4 Carbon at a ~17% lower Cost per Task<br />
<br />
@GoogleDeepMind has released Gemini 4 Neon, its fourth model in just seven weeks. Neon (xhigh) scores 61 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, 1 point ahead of Gemini 4 Carbon (high) and 3rd overall, behind GPT-6.2 Astra (max) and Claude Fable 5.2 (max). At high effort, Neon matches Carbon (high) at less than half the Cost per Task using Carbon’s standard (non-discounted) pricing.<br />
<br />
Key takeaways:<br />
<br />
➤ Slight improvement in Intelligence for lower Cost and Time per Task: Neon (xhigh) scores 61 on the Intelligence Index, 1 point ahead of Carbon (high), with small improvements in agentic knowledge work, visual reasoning and coding. It costs $6.44 per task, ~17% less than Carbon ($7.76 at non-discounted pricing), and takes ~21 minutes per task, ~40% less than Carbon (~36 minutes). Neon uses ~15% more output tokens per task, so the lower cost comes purely from its 25% lower token prices ($3/$15 vs $4/$20 per 1M input/output tokens), and the lower time from its higher output speed (~110 vs ~57 output tokens per second)<br />
<br />
➤ Competitive with Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Fable 5.2 on coding agents: With Antigravity CLI, Neon (xhigh) scores 59 on the Artificial Analysis Coding Agent Index, 3 points ahead of Carbon (high, 55). It scores in line with Claude Code with Claude Fable 5.2 (xhigh, 58) and Claude Opus 5.5 (max, 58) at a much lower Cost per Task. Gemini 4 Neon in Antigravity has an average Cost per Task of $6.86, compared to $12.61 for Claude Opus 5.5 (max) and $15.47 for Claude Fable 5.2 (xhigh). Neon is available in Antigravity CLI for Pro and Ultra subscribers<br />
<br />
➤ Significant improvements in visual reasoning and output presentation quality: In GDP.pdf, Gemini 4 Neon (high) scores 33.4%, 5 points above Carbon (high, 28.4%) and in line with the highest scores measured, while Neon (xhigh) scores 28.0%. On AA-Briefcase, Neon (xhigh) scores 1700 Elo overall and leads on Presentation Quality with 1771 Elo, 45 points ahead of the previous leader Claude Sonnet 5.5 (max, 1726). On Google’s Multimodal Knowledge Agent evaluation (MKA), Neon reaches a Climb WSR of 56%, up 2 points from Carbon and on par with the leader, Claude Fable 5.2 (max, 57%)<br />
<br />
Key model details:<br />
<br />
➤ Context Window: 1M tokens<br />
<br />
➤ Multimodality: Text, image, video, and speech input, with text output<br />
<br />
➤ Availability: Gemini 4 Neon is publicly available at launch through Google’s API. Pro and Ultra subscribers can also access it in Antigravity and the Gemini app<br />
<br />
➤ Effort levels: Gemini 4 Neon offers four effort levels (low, medium, high, and xhigh) - xhigh was introduced to Gemini models in the recent Hydrogen release<br />
<br />
➤ Pricing: $3/$15 per 1M input/output tokens, 50% above Gemini 4 Carbon’s currently discounted price of $2/$10 (which ends on November 30), and 25% below its standard $4/$20. Cached input tokens receive the same 95% discount ($0.15)</p>
